Anime Avatar & Festival Post — Prompt Generator

This tool writes ready-to-use prompts. It does NOT generate images itself — no free tool can do that fully automatically. You copy the prompt, paste it (with the player's photo) into a free AI image tool, then upload the result to your dashboard.

How to use this (one-time setup + every time after):
  1. Go to gemini.google.com and sign in with any free Google account (no payment needed)
  2. Upload the player's photo there, then paste the generated prompt below it
  3. Gemini will generate an anime-style image based on the photo + prompt
  4. Download the result, then upload it wherever you need it (Instagram, admin dashboard, etc.)
I'm recommending Gemini because it accepts a photo + text together (needed to keep the player's likeness) — tools like Bing Image Creator are text-only and won't use the real photo. I haven't verified today's exact free-tier limits on Gemini — if you hit a cap, it resets, or ask me to check current terms before relying on heavy daily use.
